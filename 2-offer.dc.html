Why Committed Government Spending Is The #1 Signal A Suburb Is About To Move (And Why Announcements Are Worthless)

Here is one of the twelve, in full, so you can see the kind of thing we mean.

Everybody knows infrastructure lifts an area. Almost nobody checks it properly.

The mistake is reacting to announcements. A government announces a train line, the local agents put it in every listing, and prices move on the announcement. Then the project gets delayed, rescoped, or quietly dropped.

A developer does not look at announcements. They look at committed money with a date attached.

That is a different document, in a different place, and it is public.

When money is committed, contracts get signed. Contractors move in. Those workers need somewhere to live. Then the services follow the workers, and the families follow the services.

That sequence takes years, and it starts long before the average buyer notices anything has changed.

That is one check. There are eleven more.

Committed spending moves an area years before the market notices.
